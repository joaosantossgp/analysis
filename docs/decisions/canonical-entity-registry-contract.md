# Canonical Entity Registry Contract

Status: proposed for João's decision.

Issue: #415. Depends on #414. Blocks the implementation work in #417, #418,
and #419. Task type: decision only; this document does not authorize DDL,
migrations, data backfill, runtime changes, or product claims.

## Decision request and recommendation

**Recommended contract:** use an append-only, relational, bitemporal entity
registry. It assigns every real-world entity an immutable opaque `entity_id`,
records sourced claims without replacing competitors, and computes a canonical
answer only through a versioned, domain-and-attribute-specific policy.

This is the smallest contract that can retain the current `companies.cd_cvm`
and Energy identifiers as external facts while safely answering historical
questions. It deliberately does not prescribe physical table names or DDL;
those belong to #417 and #418 after #416 approves the migration/release plan.

João must approve this proposed contract before Wave 1 starts. This record is
not evidence that João has approved it.

## Normative terms and common envelope

The words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, and **MAY** are normative.

Every persisted registry record or decision MUST carry a stable record ID,
creation/system timestamp, authoring origin, and audit link to its command or
source snapshot where applicable. A source-bearing record MUST retain source
class, source reference/snapshot, retrieved/observed time where available, and
the unmodified raw value/payload reference. A missing value, unavailable
source, failed source, and unsupported namespace are distinct states.

All temporal intervals use `[from, to)`: inclusive `from`, exclusive `to`;
`to = null` means open ended. Stored instants are UTC; date-only source facts
are represented as an explicit jurisdiction/date precision rather than silently
inventing a time of day.

## 1. Entities and identity

An entity is one real-world thing, not a database row, name, ticker, or source
identifier. Each entity MUST have one immutable opaque `entity_id` generated by
the registry. It MUST NOT encode source, base kind, country, legal name, or
business meaning, and MUST NOT be reassigned, edited, or reused after
retirement.

The initial closed **base-kind** vocabulary is:

| Kind | Meaning | Not interchangeable with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `organization` | One real-world organization | economic group, security, asset, or concession |
| `economic_group` | Economic/conglomerate grouping | organization, legal person, or issuer |
| `security_class` | Issued tradable class or instrument | issuer or ticker |
| `concession` | Legal/regulatory concession or service right | holder or operator |
| `plant_or_asset` | Physical operating asset/plant | parcel or legal owner |
| `market_parcel` | Market/settlement parcel or unit | underlying plant |

`legal_entity`, `issuer`, `regulated_agent`, and `vehicle` are typed,
bitemporal facets/classifications of an `organization`, not competing base
entity IDs. One organization MAY hold multiple facets simultaneously; for
example, an incorporated organization can also be an issuer and a regulated
agent. CVM/CNPJ ingestion MUST NOT create separate issuer and legal-entity
records for the same organization. Relationship roles (including owner,
operator, concession holder, and service provider) are not facets and remain
relationships under section 5.

A base kind is immutable except for a documented registry correction that does
not change the real-world thing. An attempted cross-base-kind link MUST be
blocked. New base kinds and facets require a versioned policy decision;
free-text kinds/facets are forbidden.

### Entity lifecycle

An entity has bitemporal lifecycle states `active`, `retired`, and `deprecated`.
`retired` or `deprecated` is a registry state, not a legal conclusion; legal
dissolution, where evidenced, is an assertion/facet fact with its own effective
and system time. A lifecycle transition MUST retain its reason and supporting
assertion/evidence. Historical corrections MAY be appended for any lifecycle
state, including after retirement, provided they do not erase history. New
forward effective attachments, relationships, observations, or canonical links
to a retired entity MUST block unless an approved lineage/continuity command
explicitly establishes their permitted interval. Dissolution, retirement,
deprecation, or successor lineage MUST NOT transfer identifiers, observations,
or identity automatically and MUST NOT permit `entity_id` reuse.

## 2. Identifiers, aliases, and names

An external identifier is an attachment claim, never the entity's identity. It
MUST include `namespace`, raw value, normalized value, identifier strength,
source/evidence link, effective interval, and system interval. Namespace
defines format, base-kind/facet eligibility, scope, and uniqueness; no
global normalizer or global unique index is permitted.

The following is the approved minimum initial namespace registry. All entries
retain raw value; `trim-exact/v1` means Unicode trim only, with no unproven
format coercion. `digits-only/v1` removes presentation punctuation after
official-source validation; it does not prove a value valid. Normalizer changes
are versioned.

| Namespace | Eligible base kind / required facet | Normalizer | Scope and effective-time uniqueness key | Strength and automatic identity action | Duplicate/overlap result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `br.cvm.cd_cvm` | `organization` with `issuer` facet | `trim-exact/v1` | `authority=CVM`; `(namespace, authority, normalized, effective interval)` | Strong only from accepted official CVM evidence; MAY auto-link/create only when unique | Preserve both claims; `duplicate_strong_id`, block link/creation and review |
| `br.cnpj` | `organization` with `legal_entity` facet; `vehicle` facet where evidenced | `digits-only/v1` | `country=BR`; full 14-digit normalized CNPJ and effective interval | Full CNPJ is strong only from qualifying official evidence; MAY auto-link/create only when unique. Root-only CNPJ, if represented, is weak/non-identity and never auto-links/creates | Full-ID collision blocks/reviews; root-only duplicates remain non-canonical candidates |
| `br.b3.ticker` | `security_class`; its issuer relation is separately asserted | `trim-exact/v1` | `market/exchange`, instrument/class context, normalized ticker, and effective interval | Temporal weak identifier; never creates identity by itself | Overlap in the same scope blocks/review; reuse is allowed only for non-overlapping intervals |
| `br.aneel.ceg` | `plant_or_asset` with ANEEL registration facet where evidenced | `trim-exact/v1` pending proven source contract | `authority=ANEEL`, normalized value, and effective interval | Strong only from accepted official ANEEL evidence; MAY auto-link/create only when unique | Preserve claims; collision/overlap blocks and reviews |
| `br.ccee.plant_parcel` | `market_parcel` with CCEE market-registration facet where evidenced | `trim-exact/v1` pending proven source contract | `authority=CCEE`, normalized value, and effective interval | Strong only from accepted official CCEE evidence; MAY auto-link/create only when unique | Preserve claims; collision/overlap blocks and reviews |
| `br.<authority>.regulated_agent_code` | `organization` with `regulated_agent` facet | `trim-exact/v1` until authority contract is proven | authority, regulator/program, normalized value, and effective interval | Conservative weak; MAY become strong/auto-linkable only through a later authority-specific namespace decision | Candidate conflict; no automatic identity action |
| `br.<authority>.concession_code` | `concession` | `trim-exact/v1` until authority contract is proven | authority, program, normalized value, and effective interval | Conservative weak; MAY become strong/auto-linkable only through a later authority-specific namespace decision | Candidate conflict; no automatic identity action |

No unsupported regex, check-digit rule, leading-zero rule, or source format is
invented by this ADR. A namespace implementation MUST require official-source
validation before accepting a strong ID or enabling automatic identity action.
Strong-ID collisions preserve both claims and block canonical link/creation for
review. Source-specific IDs can coexist only when this registry permits it.

Normalization MUST be deterministic, versioned by namespace, and retain raw
input. A change to normalization creates a new normalized representation and
audit event; it MUST NOT overwrite raw input or silently reassign an entity.
Identifiers with malformed values, unknown namespace, invalid effective period,
or ineligible kind MUST be rejected as typed errors.

Names and aliases are claims with type (`legal_name`, `trade_name`, `brand`,
`ticker_display`, `former_name`, `source_alias`), language/locale where known,
effective/system intervals, and evidence. A rename closes the prior name claim
and opens another on the same entity; it never changes `entity_id`. Names are
search aids and evidence targets, never sufficient proof for an automatic link
or merge.

## 3. Evidence, assertions, and conflicts

An assertion says that a subject entity has an attribute, identifier attachment,
relationship, or lineage event during an effective interval. It is append-only:
corrections supersede prior assertions in system time without deleting them. An
assertion has immutable origin/authoring route: `source_ingestion`, `user`,
`reviewer`, or `derived`. Origin records who/what authored the claim; it is not
evidence provenance and MUST NOT be overwritten by attached evidence.

An assertion has zero or more evidence links. Every evidence record has an
independent source class, source snapshot/reference, observed/retrieved time
where available, status, and raw payload reference:

| Evidence source class | Meaning | Policy eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `official_primary` | Original regulator, issuer, exchange, or authority record | Policy may select |
| `official_external` | Official dataset/republication outside primary record | Policy may select |
| `external` | Reputable third-party/provider evidence | Policy may select only if allowed |
| `derived` | Deterministic evidence derived from input lineage | Only under explicit policy |

Zero evidence is represented by `evidence_status = none`. It is neither an
evidence source class nor an assertion lifecycle state. A user-authored
assertion without evidence MUST retain `origin=user`, `evidence_status = none`,
actor, reason, and interval; it MUST NOT be labeled official or silently
substituted for a missing source. A user-authored assertion backed by official
evidence remains `origin=user` plus its independent official evidence link;
neither fact overwrites the other. Resolution eligibility evaluates assertion
state, origin route, and qualifying evidence together, including an explicit
policy route that permits `evidence_status = none`.

Assertion state is one of `draft`, `submitted`, `accepted`, `rejected`,
`superseded`, `withdrawn`, `conflicted`, or `blocked`. `accepted` means it
passed its validation/review route; it does not itself make the claim canonical.

Competing accepted claims remain queryable. A conflict records the competing
claim IDs, attribute/domain, overlap, detection time, and resolution state.
No ingestion, refresh, or reader may discard a claim merely because another
claim has a higher policy rank.

## 4. Two time axes and point-in-time reads

Effective time answers “what was true in the represented world?” System time
answers “what did this registry believe at a given time?” Every identifier,
name, assertion, relationship, policy, and lineage record MUST carry both.

A point-in-time query MUST receive `effective_as_of` and MAY receive
`system_as_of`. If omitted, `system_as_of` defaults to query execution time;
`effective_as_of` has **no implicit latest default** and callers MUST choose it.
The response MUST echo both requested times, the selected immutable policy
ID/version, and
the resolution status. A retroactive correction uses an earlier effective
interval and a later system interval, so an older `system_as_of` reproduces the
then-believed answer.

Invalid intervals (`from >= to`), unbounded historic ambiguity introduced by a
write, and overlaps prohibited by a namespace/relationship policy MUST block
the write atomically. Open intervals are allowed only where that record type
allows them.

## 5. Relationship dimensions and lineage

Relationships have subject, object, typed predicate/dimension, payload,
effective/system intervals, assertion/evidence links, and status. The contract
MUST NOT collapse these independent dimensions:

| Dimension | Example payload |
| --- | --- |
| legal ownership | owner and legal interest basis |
| voting interest | vote percentage/class |
| economic interest | cash-flow/economic percentage |
| control | controller, control basis/status |
| accounting treatment | consolidation/equity-method basis |
| operational role | owner, operator, concession holder, service provider |

A relationship can have several dimensions for the same pair and time window;
one dimension does not imply another. Numeric interest requires unit, basis,
and precision. A concession holder is not presumed owner or operator.

Lineage is distinct from relationship membership. `legal_continuation` means
the same organization with its legal-entity facet survives (for example, a
legal rename) and retains the same `entity_id`. `merged_into`, `split_from`, `spun_off_from`,
`succeeded_by`, and `acquired_from` relate **distinct** entity IDs. A merger or
spin-off MUST NOT mutate historical observations from one entity to another.
Writes that introduce a prohibited directed cycle in lineage, control, or a
policy-designated ownership graph MUST block; allowed reciprocal economic facts
must be explicitly modeled rather than bypassing cycle validation.

## 6. Canonical resolution

Canonical is a query result, not a mutable flag on an entity or assertion. A
resolution policy is versioned and binds exactly one `(domain, attribute)` pair
to: optional consumer scope, eligible base kinds/facets, assertion state/origin
routes, qualifying evidence or `evidence_status = none` routes, namespace rules where
relevant, precedence/tie behavior, temporal semantics, conflict treatment, and
override rules. Every policy version has an immutable `policy_id` and version,
plus activation/effective and system intervals. Examples include
`company.identity/cvm_id`, `company.display/legal_name`, and
`energy.distribution/control`; a generic “best source” hierarchy is forbidden.

Callers MAY pin an immutable `policy_id`/version. Otherwise, resolution MUST
select exactly one immutable policy version from the versioned policy catalog by
`domain`, `attribute`, optional consumer scope, `effective_as_of`, and
`system_as_of`. Zero eligible versions return `policy_missing`; multiple
eligible versions return `policy_ambiguous` and a blocked result. There is no
hidden default, latest, priority, or insertion ordering. Later policy insertion
or activation MUST NOT silently change a historical query: the same inputs and
the same `system_as_of` reproduce the same selected policy version. A response
MUST echo the immutable selected policy ID/version (or the explicit missing or
ambiguous status).

Resolution MUST filter claims by both time axes, apply the declared policy
version, and return one of `resolved`, `conflicted`, `ambiguous`, `unavailable`,
or `blocked`. It MUST include the selected claim IDs or conflicting candidates.
There is no latest-wins rule: later system time is neither a tie-breaker nor
proof of correctness. A policy that cannot decide without an arbitrary winner
returns `conflicted` or `ambiguous`.

User overrides are scoped to a domain/attribute/entity (and, where needed,
consumer workspace), have reason, actor, effective/system intervals, policy
version, and reversal link. They can outrank a policy only when that policy
allows user override. They never alter source assertions and must display as an
override to downstream consumers.

## 7. Application, review, and blocked states

The command layer classifies a change before applying it. A low-impact command
MAY apply immediately only when all are true: deterministic normalization;
unique eligible strong identifier; no conflict/overlap; same base kind; no
change to a canonical result outside the command’s scoped attribute; and no
affected lineage, control, legal-continuity, or published dependent result.
It still records the command, evidence, policy, and reversal path.

Mandatory review is required for name-only/fuzzy candidates, ambiguous identity,
duplicate strong IDs, non-unique source IDs, historical interval changes,
competing canonical candidates, user override, legal-continuity decision,
control/perimeter/accounting changes, merge, split, lineage change, or any
command whose impact preview reaches relationships, observations, calculations,
exports, or unresolved conflicts beyond the immediate record. Fuzzy matching
MAY create bounded ranked suggestions; it MUST NEVER create an entity link,
canonical result, merge, or split.

Blocked states are explicit and non-destructive: `duplicate_strong_id`,
`ambiguous_identity`, `invalid_interval`, `policy_missing`, `policy_ambiguous`,
`policy_unsupported`, `evidence_unavailable`, `conflict_unresolved`,
`cycle_detected`, `stale_approval`, and `impact_incomplete`. A blocked command
preserves input and diagnostics but makes no canonical mutation.

## 8. Merge, split, reversal, and continuity

A merge is a high-impact, reviewed lineage decision between distinct entities;
it MUST retain both IDs, claims, and relationships, identify the survivor only
for forward resolution, and enumerate affected identifiers, relationships,
observations, calculations, exports, and unresolved conflicts. It MUST NOT
rewrite history or delete the absorbed entity.

A split creates distinct entity IDs and lineage edges; inherited identifiers or
facts remain attached to their original claim until explicitly reasserted with
time/evidence. Reversal is a new compensating command that closes/supersedes the
prior decision in system time and restores prior resolution under the applicable
policy. It MUST NOT erase the original command. Legal continuity is decided
separately: rename/re-registration may preserve the same ID only with supporting
evidence; uncertainty remains blocked rather than being treated as a merge.

## 9. Query, storage, and audit invariants

The relational implementation MUST provide bounded, indexed access for:

- entity by immutable ID and kind;
- identifier by `(namespace, normalized_value, effective_as_of, system_as_of)`;
- names/aliases by normalized search key and entity;
- subject/object/dimension relationship windows;
- canonical attribute resolution by entity/domain/attribute/two time axes; and
- lineage and conflict/review lookup by entity and command.

Implementation MUST demonstrate representative SQLite and PostgreSQL query plans
before accepting indexes. It MUST use local persisted data and source snapshots
for normal reads; remote refresh is explicit. A read returns local stale,
unavailable, pending, or failed status rather than calling a source silently.
Registry lineage is auditable from canonical answer -> policy version -> selected
assertion -> evidence/source snapshot -> command/actor -> entity and intervals.
Graph databases, remote graph services, generic EAV, and unbounded graph/fuzzy
queries are out of scope absent a later measured decision.

## Table-driven contract examples

| Case | Required representation | Required result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rename | Same `organization` with legal-entity facet; close/open legal-name claims with evidence | Historical name resolves by effective/system time; identity remains stable |
| Ticker change | Same evidenced security class and separately related issuer organization; ticker attachment changes interval | Old ticker resolves only in its active interval; no entity change |
| Merger | Distinct entities plus reviewed `merged_into` lineage and impact preview | Both histories remain queryable; no destructive reassignment |
| Spin-off | New distinct entity plus `spun_off_from` lineage | Parent history stays with parent; child starts only as asserted |
| Control change | Control dimension claim changes interval, independent of ownership/voting/accounting | As-of read shows control basis without inferring other dimensions |
| Competing claims | Persist both claims and conflict record | Resolution returns policy-selected answer only if determinable; otherwise conflict |
| Retroactive correction | New assertion with earlier effective time and later system time supersedes prior belief | Old system-as-of reproduces former answer; current belief reflects correction |
| User override | Scoped `origin=user` assertion/override with `evidence_status = none`, reason, interval, policy | Visible as override; never presented as official evidence |

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Trade-off | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continue `cd_cvm`/CNPJ/ticker as mutable universal identity | Lowest initial change, but cannot represent assets, historical IDs, source conflicts, or lineage safely | Rejected |
| One current canonical row per entity with overwrite audit log | Simpler reads, but cannot reconstruct prior belief or preserve competing claims | Rejected |
| Universal source ranking/latest-wins | Easy ingestion, but mixes domains and makes late refreshes arbitrary winners | Rejected |
| Graph database or remote identity service | Flexible traversal, but adds deployment/offline/performance risk before measured need | Rejected |
| Append-only relational bitemporal claims and versioned policies | More explicit storage/query discipline, but supports offline audit, conflict retention, and bounded indexes | Recommended |
