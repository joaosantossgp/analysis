# CVMAnalytics v0.24.1 — Excel export period scope

## Added

- **Excel export period scope selector.** The export panel now includes a
  compact **Histórico** selector with four options: `3 anos`, `5 anos`
  (default), `10 anos`, and `Todo histórico`. The selected scope controls how
  many annual fiscal years are included in the exported workbook. (#344)

- **Current-year ITR inclusion.** When a company has quarterly ITR data for a
  year whose annual DFP has not yet been filed (e.g. 1Q26 while the 2026 DFP
  is still pending), those ITR periods are automatically appended to the
  export regardless of scope. The Model sheet shows ITR columns interleaved
  after the last closed annual year.

- **Export scope disclosed in workbook.** The `metadata` hidden sheet now
  includes `Export scope` and `ITR-only years` rows. The `README` sheet Period
  window description names the selected scope and notes any ITR-only year
  included.

## Internal

- `QueryLayer.get_available_export_periods` splits available years into annual
  DFP years and ITR-only years in a single SQL query.
- `CVMReadService.resolve_excel_export_scope` maps scope strings to concrete
  year lists and `period_window` tuples for `ExcelExporter`.
- `_pick_model_periods` now includes quarters from years strictly newer than
  the latest selected annual year, enabling the ITR-only column guarantee.
